Payment

PayPal has your payment

Send your brief to support@elghaly.dev with your PayPal transaction ID. We start once the payment shows in PayPal.

What happens next

Payment confirmed + brief received → binary on your side in 5–7 days. If your order is ready sooner, we'll let you know right away. The clock starts when both are in; an incomplete brief pauses it.

Your brief: the seat you paid for, the pairs you want (1–2 on Starter, named pairs on Pro or Source), and a reachable email. Put the PayPal transaction ID in the same message so we can match it.

Refunds follow /refunds. A PayPal payment can also be disputed through PayPal.
